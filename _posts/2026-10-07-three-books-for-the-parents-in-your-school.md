Classical schools thrive when parents understand what their children are being given. Classical Academic Press has three short books that help, and they're good choices for new-family gifts, parent nights, book studies, admissions events, or the school library.

<img src="/assets/blog/cap-parent-books/perrin-intro-classical-education.jpg" alt="Cover of An Introduction to Classical Education: A Guide for Parents by Christopher Perrin" class="my-6 w-48 rounded-sm shadow-lg">

**An Introduction to Classical Education: A Guide for Parents** (revised), by Dr. Christopher Perrin. This is the clearest starting point for new and current families. The new edition adds expanded definitions, fresh stories, charts, and an update on the classical movement.

<img src="/assets/blog/cap-parent-books/mccurdy-raising-sturdy-kids.jpg" alt="Cover of Raising Sturdy Kids by Keith A. McCurdy" class="my-6 w-48 rounded-sm shadow-lg">

**Raising Sturdy Kids** (new), by Keith A. McCurdy. A practical, encouraging guide to raising resilient children who can do hard things and grow through healthy struggle.

<img src="/assets/blog/cap-parent-books/wisdom-and-eloquence-for-parents.jpg" alt="Cover of Wisdom and Eloquence for Parents by Robert Littlejohn, Charles T. Evans, and Keith A. McCurdy" class="my-6 w-48 rounded-sm shadow-lg">

**Wisdom and Eloquence for Parents**, by Robert Littlejohn, Charles T. Evans, and Keith A. McCurdy. This one explains the deeper purpose of classical Christian education, which is forming wise, eloquent, faithful young men and women.
