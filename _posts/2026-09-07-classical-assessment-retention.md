How should an Orthodox classical school assess students — and help them retain what they have learned? In this staff training session at **St. Andrew Academy**, Reader Dionysius Buhler takes up classical assessment and retention as a matter of formation, not merely measurement.

The talk is offered here for teachers, school leaders, and homeschool parents who want practical help thinking through exams, memory, and the long work of keeping knowledge in the soul.
